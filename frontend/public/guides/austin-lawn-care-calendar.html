Austin Lawn Care Calendar

Use this calendar as a starting point for Bermuda, St. Augustine and Zoysia lawns. Adjust the work to your grass, shade, soil, rainfall and current conditions.

Before you startThis is general guidance, not a fixed treatment schedule. Watering rules change and differ by provider. Check your current local restrictions before watering; new sod may need an approved variance. Follow product labels and ask a qualified professional when needed.

Good habits all year

  • Mow according to growth and avoid removing more than one-third of the leaf height at once.
  • Water only when needed and allowed; skip irrigation when rain has supplied enough moisture.
  • Do not fertilize dormant or drought-stressed warm-season grass. Consider a soil test to guide nutrient decisions.

Winter to early spring

January

Observe and plan

  • Clear heavy leaf buildup and debris when the ground is not saturated or frozen.
  • Dormant warm-season grass can turn brown in winter; avoid pushing growth with fertilizer.
  • Check tools, sharpen mower blades and note areas with drainage or weed concerns.

February

Prepare, do not rush

  • Identify your grass type and consider a soil test before planning fertilizer.
  • If crabgrass has been a problem, ask about pre-emergent timing before it germinates. Choose only a product labeled for your grass and follow its directions.
  • Water only when needed and permitted by current local rules.

March

Watch for green-up

  • Mow as the lawn starts growing; adjust frequency rather than cutting too short.
  • Check sprinklers for leaks, overspray and runoff when testing is permitted.
  • Wait until warm-season grass is actively growing and has been mowed at least twice before considering spring nitrogen.

Spring to early summer

April

Support active growth

  • Use soil-test guidance and grass condition to decide whether fertilizer is needed; avoid applying before heavy rain.
  • Keep mower blades sharp and follow the one-third rule as growth increases.
  • Check your provider's watering schedule and adjust irrigation for rainfall.

May

Build healthy habits

  • Mow on the schedule your lawn's growth actually requires.
  • For compacted soil, consider core aeration while warm-season grass is actively growing and conditions allow recovery. Avoid aerating stressed or newly established turf.
  • Watch for runoff or puddling and adjust irrigation without exceeding local restrictions.

June

Prepare for heat

  • Check moisture and recent rainfall before watering, and stay within local rules.
  • Watch for wilting, footprints that remain in the grass or spreading brown patches; the cause may need investigation.
  • Avoid fertilizing grass that is dormant or stressed by heat and drought.

Summer to early fall

July

Manage heat stress

  • Let mowing frequency follow growth; do not scalp the lawn during hot, dry weather.
  • Water only when needed and permitted by your water provider. Avoid runoff onto pavement.
  • Investigate persistent damage before choosing a treatment; brown grass can have several causes.

August

Protect the lawn

  • Continue mowing carefully and avoid fertilizer on drought-stressed or dormant turf.
  • If winter weeds have been a recurring problem, ask about fall pre-emergent timing and products labeled for your grass.
  • Check irrigation equipment and follow any updated watering restrictions.

September

Plan for cooler weather

  • Remove heavy leaf buildup and adjust mowing as growth changes.
  • If considering late-season nitrogen, apply only while the grass is actively growing; consult local extension guidance and allow at least six weeks before the expected first frost.
  • Choose weed-control products carefully for your grass and follow all label directions.

Fall to winter

October

Ease into fall

  • Keep leaves from forming a thick, wet mat over the grass.
  • Mow only as growth requires and reduce irrigation as weather and rainfall change.
  • Avoid pushing late growth as warm-season turf approaches dormancy.

November

Prepare for cold

  • Clear accumulated leaves and debris, especially after wet weather.
  • Review irrigation settings and local freeze-protection advice; follow the current rules for your provider.
  • Do not fertilize dormant warm-season turf.

December

Rest and review

  • Keep heavy traffic and equipment off frozen or saturated grass where practical.
  • Clean tools and make a list of repairs, drainage issues and lawn goals for next year.
  • Check current watering restrictions if dry conditions make watering necessary.
