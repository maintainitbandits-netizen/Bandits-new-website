This focused review records the service-city and lawn-calendar work and the follow-up custom-quote changes. It is not a guarantee that every fact or external integration on the site is correct.

- Service-city pages now ask about the selected service rather than reusing a lawn-care introduction and landmarks. Actual scope and availability are discussed per property; rental pages remain separate.
