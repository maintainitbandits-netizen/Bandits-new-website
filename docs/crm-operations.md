# CRM daily operations

The controls below use the existing shared CRM and outbox. The usual Vercel preview uses the **live Supabase project**, so a confirmed completion or deletion there affects shared CRM records. Automated tests use fictional in-memory records and do not delete live data. Keep a private backup before destructive changes; JSON backups do not contain photo files. Refresh every team's CRM page after an update so all devices use the same safeguards.

## Organizing jobs by work category

**Tree Trimming**, **Cleaning** and **Maintenance** are separate CRM tabs showing the same job records, not copies. On a phone, swipe the section bar sideways to find them. Each tab starts with the **Open** status filter (which excludes Paid); choose **All** or **Paid** to see paid work. Jobs still appear in the main **Jobs** list, subject to its own status filter, and in Route, Completed, estimates and other existing views as applicable.

Existing jobs are not classified from their service or description. To assign one, tap **Edit job** in **Jobs**, choose **Work category**, and save. Choose a different category or **Uncategorized** later to move it between sections. Category and service are separate: changing a category does not change the service. A job can have one category at a time. To start a new job from a category tab, use its **+ … job** button (or the top **+ Job** while on that tab); the category and matching service start selected and can be changed before saving. A new job from another tab starts uncategorized. The service choices also include manual options for the website's landscaping, turnover, cleaning and seasonal work; picking one does not assign a category.

### Assign several existing jobs

1. Wait for **Synced across devices** and use one device at a time. From **Jobs**, tap **Assign categories to jobs**; from a category tab, tap **Assign jobs to …** to preselect that destination. The category tab shortcut still lets you find jobs outside that tab. Nothing is selected or changed automatically.
2. The picker starts with **Uncategorized** and the current page's status filter. **Open** excludes Paid; choose **All** to include paid jobs. Search client, property, service, date, status, category, notes, work description, work-order text or AppFolio order number; the search runs on this device. A short team-only description is shown when available. Change **Current work category** to move already-categorized jobs, find unrecognized categories, or clear a category. Use individual checkboxes or **Select shown**.
3. Selections remain selected when hidden by another search or filter. Check the count and expand **Review all selected**, which includes hidden jobs; remove individual jobs there or use **Clear selection**. Choose the destination (including **Uncategorized** to remove a category) and **Review and assign**, then read and confirm the number, hidden-selection warning and examples. Cancel makes no changes. Jobs already in the destination are skipped.
4. The operation changes only each selected job's work category. Jobs remain in the main Jobs list, subject to its status filter; clients, services, dates, prices, notes, AppFolio details, estimates and photo files are not changed. If a selected job or its client changed, close the picker and select again. If device storage is unavailable, free space or select fewer jobs before retrying.
5. Keep the page open until **Synced across devices** and check the relevant tab. The outbox queues individual record updates and retries failed syncs, so a batch can be partially synced while offline. Sync is **not a cross-device transaction**; simultaneous edits on other devices can still race and overwrite data despite the stale-selection check. Use one device at a time, and keep a private backup before large changes. JSON backups do not include photos. A hosted preview is connected to the live CRM; do not confirm test changes there unless you intend to change real jobs.

PDF review lets you select a category per work order or apply one to the batch, then adjust individual entries. Address import can assign a single category to its new jobs; the weekly planner supports a default and per-job changes; scheduling from an estimate also offers a category. These start uncategorized unless you choose one. Repeating jobs inherit their category, while the existing rules for AppFolio details and date requirements still apply. Categories use the normal CRM sync and are included in private JSON backups. No category changes occur merely by opening a tab.

Each category tab's **Select … jobs to delete** picker is limited to that category and uses its current status filter; the main Jobs picker can select jobs from all categories. Review the selection and confirmation carefully. Deletion still removes the shared job and does not delete photo files. Use one device at a time and wait for **Synced across devices**.

## Properties, job prices and repeats

- A property card shows its earliest Scheduled job (including any overdue job), its repeat and price. **Edit next job** changes that specific job. If there is no scheduled job and just one unscheduled job, its repeat and price appear with **Edit unscheduled job**. Expand **View all scheduled and unscheduled jobs** to find another job. Jobs with no explicit property appear under the client's first property, matching the address used for routing. **+ Job** creates a separate job; it does not edit an existing one. Check the open-jobs list before creating another job for the same work.
- Select **Every 2 weeks (biweekly)** and enter the price on the job, then **Save**. A visible message distinguishes **saved on this device, waiting to sync** from **synced across devices**. Keep the page open and wait for sync; if device storage fails, use **Backup** before leaving. If a save reports an uncertain result, check Jobs and sync before trying again to avoid a duplicate. If the job or its original client changes while the editor is open, saving is blocked; close and reopen the job to review the latest record. This check is not an atomic transaction across devices, so use one device at a time.
- You may choose a repeat for an Unscheduled job without entering a date. It stays Unscheduled and off routes until you explicitly schedule it. After a scheduled repeating job is marked Done, Invoiced or Paid, the next occurrence is created once, with the same repeat and price. Other already-scheduled jobs are not updated automatically; review and edit each one as needed. An unrecognized legacy repeat must be explicitly changed to a supported option before saving. Unrecognized legacy service values are preserved unless changed.

## Completed work

Open **Completed** for a searchable view of jobs whose current status is **Done**, **Invoiced**, or **Paid**. Existing finished jobs appear automatically; this view does not create a second record or alter the jobs. Filter by status or search the client, property, service, work category, or scheduled date. Dates shown and the newest-first order use the scheduled job date; the CRM does not record a completion date here.

To mark scheduled work finished, tap **→ Done** on the Jobs card, **Mark Done** on Route, or use the confirmed **Mark done & next** action in next-stop mode. A repeating job can create its next scheduled occurrence using the existing repeat behavior. Done and Invoiced jobs also remain in **Jobs → Open** until marked Paid, so unpaid work is not hidden; Paid jobs remain in Completed and Jobs → All. Marking a job Done does not automatically send an invoice or contact a customer.

Completed cards use the existing job editor, Invoice, Before / after photos, and Delete job controls. If a job's client is missing, editing from Completed is blocked rather than silently selecting another client; restore or review the client first. Deleting a job still requires confirmation and does not delete its private photo files. Wait for **Synced across devices** and use one device at a time; the existing outbox retries unsynced changes. No live records are used in automated tests.

## Work-order PDF import

Use **Import work order PDFs** to select and review multiple AppFolio PDFs. Files are read on the device; only confirmed job details sync to the shared CRM. An order with no planned date is saved as **Unscheduled** and stays off routes until you explicitly schedule it. This is a manual import, not an AppFolio connection: it does not accept work or sync changes back. See [Importing AppFolio work-order PDFs](crm-work-order-pdfs.md) for privacy, limits, duplicate handling and important format/testing limitations.

## Due and overdue jobs

Open **Due & overdue** to see scheduled or unscheduled jobs with due-by or exact-day requirements. The dashboard compares the required date to the device's current local date: earlier dates are overdue, today is due today, and later dates are upcoming. A job can be overdue even when its planned appointment was earlier. Missing/invalid required dates or rules appear under **Needs a valid requirement** rather than being silently assigned a deadline; a required date without a valid rule blocks quick routing or moving until corrected. Jobs without a requirement and jobs no longer Scheduled or Unscheduled are excluded. Tap **Edit job** to review or change a requirement or estimate; the dashboard does not automatically reschedule or complete anything.

Optional estimated work time is entered in whole minutes, from 1 to 10,080, or left blank. It is a team-provided estimate, not a promise; travel, breaks and live traffic are excluded. Repeat occurrences inherit the work estimate, but not the old occurrence's date requirement. For weekly workload and unknown-time behavior, see [CRM workweek planner](crm-week-planner.md).

## Customer price estimates and job quotes

The optional **Photo estimate with AI** prepares an unsaved, reviewable estimate from selected photos and a work description. It can offer an unverified total price range, and follow-up answers can be sent to update it with separate consent. Gemini 3.8 Flash is the default; Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is an optional, manually selected alternative whose quality and availability may differ. Switching models clears consent; there is no automatic model switch or retry. If an update stalls, the user can stop waiting and manually review the previous draft while the window stays open; Gemini may still process or count the stopped request, and the old scope and price will not reflect changed answers. Nothing is saved by opening the editor, and closing the photo window discards the unsaved draft. It requires a Gemini API Free-tier project; this app cannot verify the tier and paid billing may incur charges. The endpoint is disabled by default until configured. Client/property fields are not separately sent, but photos and entered text go to Google. Google may use free-tier content to improve products and human reviewers may process it; Google says not to submit personal, sensitive or confidential information. Review the privacy, free-tier prerequisite, pricing and setup details in [AI photo estimates](crm-ai-photo-estimates.md) before enabling or using it.

- Open **Estimates** to create a customer price estimate independently of the work-time estimates on jobs. Choose a client, service and estimate date, and enter your own descriptions, quantities and rates. A flat price is a single line with quantity 1. Up to 50 lines are supported; quantities can have three decimal places and rates two. Each line is rounded to the nearest cent before the total is added. Prices cannot be negative. Tax is **not** calculated automatically; add it as a line if needed. Nothing is sent automatically, and Draft, Sent, Accepted and Declined are manual tracking statuses, not messages to the customer.
- **View** opens a customer-facing estimate; use **Copy text** or **Print / PDF** to share it yourself. Customer notes appear on that document. Team-only notes do not appear on the estimate or on a job created from it, but they are still stored in the shared CRM and in JSON backups. Customer name, property and notes may contain personal information. All authenticated CRM team accounts currently have access to shared records; there is no per-user or per-client isolation.
- The same page lists **Quotes from existing jobs**, generated from the jobs' current details and price, not separately saved quote snapshots. Existing job notes appear on those documents. The regular Jobs tab still opens a Quote for a scheduled job and an Invoice for other job statuses. Opening or copying either document does not send it. If you have not set your own rate card, the job editor shows placeholder starter prices; update **Rates** and verify the job's prices before sharing a quote.
- When ready, use **Schedule job** on a saved estimate, enter a date and confirm the location and final confirmation. The CRM must say **Synced across devices**. This creates one new Scheduled job with a fresh ID, the estimate's line items, total and customer notes; it does not change the estimate status or send anything. The linked job and estimate are independent afterward. A linked job is shown instead of another scheduling button to reduce accidental duplicates. Estimates can be edited, but reassigning one to another client is blocked while it has linked jobs for a different client; create a new estimate or review and update those jobs first. A kept estimate whose client was deleted can be edited and reassigned to an existing client.
- Record-level sync is **not transactional across devices**. Use one device at a time, wait for **Synced across devices**, and verify a newly scheduled job before trying again on another device; simultaneous actions can still race or create duplicates. Refresh every device after an update. Estimate data uses the existing `records` table and its shared access policy; no new database table or photo-storage change is required.

## Backups and restore

**Backup → Copy** includes clients, jobs, saved estimates and settings in private JSON. It does not include photo files. Restoring a backup that contains an `estimates` collection replaces the current estimates as well as the other records, after confirmation. An older backup without that collection keeps the current estimates; because it still replaces clients and jobs, some kept estimates may show a deleted client. An invalid estimates collection is rejected. Review the warning, keep a separate private backup first, and do not restore while another device is editing.

## Deleting records

- Wait until **Synced across devices** and use one device at a time. In **Jobs**, **Completed**, **Clients**, or **Estimates → Saved customer estimates**, tap the **Select … to delete** button. Search or filter, check records individually or use **Select shown**, and review the count and **Review all selected** list before confirming. Nothing is preselected. Selections hidden by a different search or filter remain selected and are counted; use **Clear selection** or remove individual entries from the review list to change them. The picker starts with the current page's search and status filter where available. Completed's picker only includes Done, Invoiced and Paid jobs. Cancel makes no changes.
- The Pipeline **Select leads to delete** control continues to select Lead-stage client records. The client picker includes all stages and displays linked-job and saved-estimate counts. A confirmed lead/client deletion also deletes **all linked jobs**, including scheduled and historical/paid jobs. If there are saved customer estimates, you must choose **keep** or **delete** them. Kept estimates retain the saved client name, property and notes, including team-only notes; they remain visible with the client marked deleted. One choice applies to the whole selection—delete separate groups to make different choices, and choose again if the selection changes.
- Deleting jobs leaves clients and saved estimates unchanged. Deleting saved estimates leaves linked jobs and their job-based quotes unchanged; the estimate picker does not select those job-based quotes. Individual client and job cards also have delete controls, as do their editors; a job can be edited or deleted directly from Route. All deletions require confirmation and cannot be undone from the CRM. Review the counts and records first.
- **Job and client deletion does not delete associated photo files.** Private files will no longer be visible in the CRM after their job is removed. If photos should be removed, use each job's Before / after photos controls before deleting its job or client. The JSON backup does not include photo files. See [CRM job photos](crm-job-photos.md); live signed-in and unsigned Storage tests are still required before relying on photo operations.
- **Properties → Delete property** removes only the property from a client, not the client's jobs. Jobs with an explicit property retain their stored address. If a job has a blank property and relies on the client's first property as its fallback address, changing that first property is blocked until those jobs have explicit addresses. The same protection applies when editing a client or importing its first property. This prevents silently redirecting jobs to a different address.
- The CRM rechecks its local shared snapshot at confirmation and queues record deletions for sync; a changed selection, changed linked records or insufficient local storage blocks the operation. Records with invalid IDs, or clients with invalid linked records, cannot be selected until reviewed. Watch the sync indicator and keep the page open until it is synced. Failed writes remain in the outbox and retry. Records are sent individually, **not as a database transaction**; a partial batch can be visible on another device until the remaining deletes sync. Simultaneous edits on another device can still race, so use one device and verify the results on the other after syncing.

For navigation, completion, date protections, temporary route progress and location sharing, see [CRM driving routes](crm-driving-routes.md#follow-the-next-stop).
