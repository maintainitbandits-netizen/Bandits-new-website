# CRM daily operations

The controls below use the existing shared CRM and outbox. The usual Vercel preview uses the **live Supabase project**, so a confirmed completion or deletion there affects shared CRM records. Automated tests use fictional in-memory records and do not delete live data. Keep a private backup before destructive changes; JSON backups do not contain photo files. Refresh every team's CRM page after an update so all devices use the same safeguards.

## Due and overdue jobs

Open **Due & overdue** to see scheduled jobs with due-by or exact-day requirements. The dashboard compares the required date to the device's current local date: earlier dates are overdue, today is due today, and later dates are upcoming. A job can be overdue even when its planned appointment was earlier. Missing/invalid required dates or rules appear under **Needs a valid requirement** rather than being silently assigned a deadline; a required date without a valid rule blocks quick routing or moving until corrected. Jobs without a requirement and jobs no longer Scheduled are excluded. Tap **Edit job** to review or change a requirement or estimate; the dashboard does not automatically reschedule or complete anything.

Optional estimated work time is entered in whole minutes, from 1 to 10,080, or left blank. It is a team-provided estimate, not a promise; travel, breaks and live traffic are excluded. Repeat occurrences inherit the work estimate, but not the old occurrence's date requirement. For weekly workload and unknown-time behavior, see [CRM workweek planner](crm-week-planner.md).

## Deleting records

- Wait until **Synced across devices** and use one device at a time. The Pipeline **Select leads to delete** control lets you check multiple Lead-stage client records, search and select shown records. Hidden selections remain selected and count toward the total. The list shows each lead's linked-job count. The final confirmation identifies the selected client count, linked-job count, sample names, and consequences. Cancel makes no changes.
- A confirmed lead/client deletion also deletes **all linked jobs**, including scheduled and historical/paid jobs. Individual client and job cards also have delete controls, as do their editors; a job can be edited or deleted directly from Route. Job deletion requires a confirmation. These deletions cannot be undone from the CRM. Make sure the count is correct and review any records you need first.
- **Job and client deletion does not delete associated photo files.** Private files will no longer be visible in the CRM after their job is removed. If photos should be removed, use each job's Before / after photos controls before deleting its job or client. The JSON backup does not include photo files. See [CRM job photos](crm-job-photos.md); live signed-in and unsigned Storage tests are still required before relying on photo operations.
- **Properties → Delete property** removes only the property from a client, not the client's jobs. Jobs with an explicit property retain their stored address. If a job has a blank property and relies on the client's first property as its fallback address, changing that first property is blocked until those jobs have explicit addresses. The same protection applies when editing a client or importing its first property. This prevents silently redirecting jobs to a different address.
- The CRM rechecks its local shared snapshot at confirmation and queues record deletions for sync; a changed selection or insufficient local storage blocks the operation. Watch the sync indicator and keep the page open until it is synced. Failed writes remain in the outbox and retry. Records are sent individually, **not as a database transaction**, so edits made simultaneously on another device can still race; use one device and verify the results on the other after syncing.
