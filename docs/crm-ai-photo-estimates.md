# CRM AI photo estimates

This feature prepares a **suggestion for a person to review**, not an automatically approved or sent quote. The endpoint is disabled by default until an administrator configures Cloudflare Workers Free and the required server-only settings. An owner reported that an earlier preview returned questions but no price. The new answer and ballpark-price workflow has been tested with fictional data and mocked providers only; the updated Cloudflare response contract, price quality, multi-photo behavior, allowance per draft, and physical-phone workflow have **not** yet been verified live.

## Preparing a draft

1. Wait for **Synced across devices**, open **Estimates → Photo estimate with AI**, and choose an existing client and a service. Client and optional property selections stay in the CRM and are not separately sent to Cloudflare. If names or addresses appear in the description, rate names, answers or images, however, those details are sent as part of that material. Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive information, and obtain any needed permission to share the photos.
2. Describe the work and provide known measurements, access details and other conditions; choose **1–3** photos, each no larger than 20 MB. Thumbnails show what was chosen. JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC/HEIF are accepted if the device can decode them; if a HEIC photo fails, choose a JPEG or PNG. Pictures are resized to at most 1,200 pixels on the long edge and re-encoded as JPEG on the device; embedded metadata is not copied. Text, addresses, people and other identifying details *visible in the picture* remain visible. The browser sends the re-encoded images, not the filenames or original files. Prepared JPEGs are bounded at 750,000 bytes each so the JSON request fits below the Vercel Function payload limit.
3. **A rate card is not required for an AI ballpark.** Original starter rates are placeholders and are not automatically used. Optionally replace the entire card in **Rates** with verified prices (`name | price`, one line per rate, no duplicates, up to two decimal places), then confirm that it is current in the photo form. Only names and numeric identifiers are sent to Cloudflare, not prices (unless a name itself contains a price). Rate matches are mapped to saved prices on the device only for the **itemized** editor path. A name match, unit, quantity or applicability can still be wrong. A changed rate card invalidates its transferred prices as before. The AI's total ballpark is an independent guess, not the rate card total.
4. Read and check the disclosure for **each request**, then tap **Generate draft**. This sends the prepared photos, work description, service and (only if selected) rate names to **Cloudflare Workers AI**. No request is sent by choosing photos, and no customer is contacted. The app does not automatically retry or fall back to another provider. Closing after sending cannot guarantee the provider stops processing or using the account's allowance; a paid account may incur charges. If the response is missing, check Workers AI usage before deliberately trying again.
5. The result may show an **AI total ballpark** (low–high) and a short basis, or say there is not enough information for a useful range. Prices are unsourced AI guesses, **not** verified local market prices, actual company rates, or final quotes. Check every assumption, exclusion, scope and amount. Prices may be very wrong; inspection and the team's judgment remain necessary. The model can still hallucinate despite instructions and validation.
6. Enter answers to any displayed questions and/or additional details. Unknowns may be left blank. To recalculate, check the separate consent box and tap **Update draft with answers**. This sends the answers and additional details **plus the photos, original description, service and selected rate names again** to Cloudflare, and each update can use more allowance. You can correct earlier answers on a later update. No request is sent merely by typing, and nothing is saved. If there are unsent changes to answers, update them or clear/revert them before opening the editor, so an old price is not mistaken for an updated one. Up to 18 answers are accepted per request; each can contain up to 500 characters, with up to 1,000 additional characters in the extra-details field.
7. A numeric line quantity absent from the person's description, answers and additional details is cleared for manual entry; type known quantities with digits, not just words. Numerical claims absent from that supplied text are also withheld from the summary, price basis, line descriptions/bases and cautions; model questions may still contain numbers. This is a limited check: spelled-out numbers, matching numbers in the wrong context or unit, and unsupported nonnumeric claims may still be wrong. Photos cannot reliably reveal size, measurements, hidden damage, safe access, site restrictions, supplies or all required work. Take measurements or inspect as needed.
8. When a range is available, **Use midpoint as flat draft** opens an unsaved estimate with one flat line: quantity 1 means one job, and its editable price is the midpoint of the AI range, rounded to the nearest cent. The line does not represent a measured area or item quantity. The AI scope, suggested lines and uncertainties go to team-only notes, not separate priced lines. Alternatively, **Review in estimate editor** opens the suggested itemized lines, using verified rate-card matches only when explicitly selected; unmatched prices remain blank. It does not distribute the AI total across line items. Review and edit the scope, quantities, prices, customer notes and total; tax is not automatic. Confirm the review checkbox and explicitly **Save estimate**. If anything changes after checking it, review and check it again. Saving does not send or schedule anything; **View → Copy text / Print / PDF** and scheduling are separate manual actions.

The ballpark, AI summary, questions, answers, extra details, cautions, line explanations and work description are copied to editable **team-only notes** in the editor, not customer-facing notes. Team-only means not shown on the estimate document; all authenticated CRM team accounts have broad access to shared records, and a saved estimate and its team-only notes are included in CRM JSON backups. If the notes exceed 5,000 characters, the app warns before shortening them. Cancelling the suggestion/editor does not create a CRM estimate. AI-selected photos are not saved as job photos or included in backups; they are kept in the open photo dialog's memory for consented follow-ups, then cleared on close. The separate Before / after job-photo feature is unchanged. Existing job quotes are not changed by generating a photo draft.

## Free-plan prerequisite, privacy and access

- **Use and maintain an account on Workers Free. The application cannot verify the account's current billing plan.** Its confirmation setting is an administrator's assertion, not a provider-side spending limit. If the account is paid, later upgraded, or otherwise configured for paid inference, charges may apply. The owner must check the Cloudflare dashboard before enabling and remain responsible for the plan and account settings. Do not upgrade or enable any paid service without separate approval; disable this feature and revoke the token if the account no longer meets the Free prerequisite.
- Cloudflare currently describes a shared **10,000 Neurons per day** Workers AI allowance, resetting at **00:00 UTC**; on Workers Free, further operations fail when the allowance is reached. A Workers Paid account also has an allowance but bills overage. Other Workers AI use on the same account shares the allowance; model capacity and rate limits can also cause errors. Photos, output size, follow-up requests and provider changes affect usage, so the number of drafts per day is unknown. See current [Workers AI pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/pricing/) and [model availability](https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-07-28-models-require-workers-paid/).
- This code calls Cloudflare's direct Workers AI REST endpoint for the vision model `@cf/google/gemma-4-26b-a4b-it`, listed for Workers Free when implemented. It does **not** use an AI Gateway or prepaid gateway credits, automatically upgrade, retry, or fall back to a different provider. An earlier preview reportedly returned questions, but the revised pricing/follow-up contract and multiple-photo behavior still require an approved live test.
- Cloudflare says it does **not train models on Workers AI customer content** and that standard stateless inference inputs and outputs are not retained after processing, subject to safety, security and legal exceptions. Stateful features, logging and caching can differ; see [Cloudflare's current data policy](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage/). This request uses `store: false`. Vercel also receives the request; the application code does not intentionally log or persist the photos or provider request. Review providers' current policies before sending customer information.
- The server checks the Supabase bearer token against the CRM project and rejects anonymous or unverified users. **Every authenticated CRM team user can use the feature once enabled.** There is no durable per-user quota or application-level rate limiter; a user with valid CRM access can call the endpoint directly and exhaust the shared allowance. Protect CRM accounts and monitor Workers AI usage. The disclosure checkbox is a user-interface confirmation, not a defense against a malicious authenticated user.

## Administrator setup

A branch-scoped Preview setup has been configured for this work; its sensitive values have not been independently verified. **Production publication and production configuration require separate approval.** No provider secret should be pasted into the CRM, repository, issue, pull request, screenshots, logs, or chat.

1. Confirm the owner has approved the intended deployment and review the code. Until configured, requests fail closed without reaching Cloudflare. A Vercel preview can share the **live CRM database**; do not save, delete, or otherwise modify live records as a test.
2. In the Cloudflare dashboard, select an account and verify that its **Workers plan is Free**, not Workers Paid or an enterprise/other paid arrangement. Prefer a dedicated Free account with no linked payment method if the owner's circumstances permit; verify eligibility and current terms in Cloudflare. Do not create or enable a paid subscription, paid gateway, credits or auto-purchases. The app cannot enforce or re-check the billing plan and will not notice a later upgrade.
3. Follow Cloudflare's [REST API setup](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/get-started/rest-api/) to obtain the selected account ID and create a **Workers AI API token** scoped to that account and only the needed permission. The token is a secret; use an API token, not the global API key. The account ID must be the 32-character hexadecimal ID.
4. In the **primary frontend Vercel project**, privately enter `CRM_CLOUDFLARE_AI_TOKEN` as a **sensitive server-only environment variable** and `CRM_CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID` as a server-only variable. Once the owner has verified and committed to keeping the account on Workers Free, set both `CRM_AI_FREE_PLAN_CONFIRMED` and `AI_QUOTES_ENABLED` to the exact value `true` for the intended deployment environment. Neither switch checks Cloudflare's plan. Missing/invalid credentials or either switch not exactly `true` prevent the provider request. Never use `REACT_APP_` variables or expose the token to the browser. Vercel environment changes require a new deployment.
5. With the owner's approval, use a small **fictional, non-sensitive photo** live test to verify the new response, follow-up and price behavior; inspect the Cloudflare dashboard's plan and Neuron usage. Test the real phone workflow separately without altering real customer records. Mock tests cannot prove provider JSON compatibility, multi-image behavior, quality, usage, network timeouts or production configuration.
6. Monitor usage and plan status. To stop new use, set `AI_QUOTES_ENABLED` to `false` (and/or remove the confirmation), redeploy, and revoke the token in Cloudflare if an immediate provider-side cutoff is needed. In-flight requests may still finish. If a quota, capacity, setup or provider error occurs, no estimate is saved and there is no automatic retry or paid fallback.

The Vercel function bounds request size, requires explicit consent in the payload, sends no server credentials to the browser, independently validates provider output and returns a no-store response. These controls do not eliminate prompt injection, estimation errors, provider retention, spending risk, or the existing CRM's shared-team access model. Use one device at a time for saves and wait for the existing sync indicator; cross-device writes are not transactional.

## Switching providers later

The provider-specific request and response handling is isolated in `frontend/lib/crm-quote-cloudflare.js`; CRM validation, the estimate editor, and saved estimate records use provider-independent fields. A future Gemini integration would require its own code, privacy/cost disclosure, credentials and approval; it is **not** configured as a fallback. Switching or disabling the provider does not itself delete saved estimates. Review current Gemini terms first: Google says it may use free-tier inputs and outputs to improve products.
