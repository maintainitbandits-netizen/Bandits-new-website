# CRM AI photo estimates

This feature prepares a **suggestion for a person to review**, not an automatically approved or sent quote. This revision uses the Google Gemini Developer API and is **disabled by default** until an administrator configures the required server-only settings for a Free-tier project. The Gemini adapter, follow-up flow, and prices have been tested with fictional data and mocked providers only; its real response contract, price quality, multiple-photo behavior, usage, and physical-phone workflow have **not** yet been verified live.

## Preparing a draft

1. Wait for **Synced across devices**, open **Estimates → Photo estimate with AI**, and choose an existing client and a service. Client and optional property selections stay in the CRM and are not separately sent to Gemini. Anything visible in a photo or entered in the description, rate names or answers is sent. **Google says not to submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to its unpaid services.** Use only material that satisfies that restriction, even if the customer has given permission. A photo of a property can itself identify a person or contain confidential details; do not assume removing metadata anonymizes it.
2. Describe the work and provide known measurements, access details and other conditions; choose **1–3** photos, each no larger than 20 MB. Thumbnails show what was chosen. JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC/HEIF are accepted if the device can decode them; if a HEIC photo fails, choose a JPEG or PNG. Pictures are resized to at most 1,200 pixels on the long edge and re-encoded as JPEG on the device; embedded metadata is not copied. Text, addresses, people and other identifying details *visible in the picture* remain visible. The browser sends the re-encoded images, not the filenames or original files. Prepared JPEGs are bounded at 750,000 bytes each so the JSON request fits below the Vercel Function payload limit.
3. **A rate card is not required for an AI ballpark.** Original starter rates are placeholders and are not automatically used. Optionally replace the entire card in **Rates** with verified prices (`name | price`, one line per rate, no duplicates, up to two decimal places), then confirm that it is current in the photo form. Only names and numeric identifiers are sent to Gemini, not prices (unless a name itself contains a price). Do not send rate names containing personal, confidential or sensitive information. Rate matches are mapped to saved prices on the device only for the **itemized** editor path. A name match, unit, quantity or applicability can still be wrong. A changed rate card invalidates its transferred prices as before. The AI's total ballpark is an independent guess, not the rate card total.
4. Read and check the disclosure for **each request**, then tap **Generate draft**. This sends the prepared photos, work description, service and (only if selected) rate names through **Vercel to Google Gemini**. Google may use free-tier inputs and outputs to improve its products, and human reviewers may process them. No request is sent by choosing photos, and no customer is contacted. The app does not automatically retry or fall back to another provider. Closing after sending cannot guarantee the provider stops processing or using the project's allowance; a paid project may incur charges. If the response is missing, check Gemini usage before deliberately trying again.
5. The result may show an **AI total ballpark** (low–high) and a short basis, or say there is not enough information for a useful range. Prices are unsourced AI guesses, **not** verified local market prices, actual company rates, or final quotes. Check every assumption, exclusion, scope and amount. Prices may be very wrong; inspection and the team's judgment remain necessary. The model can still hallucinate despite instructions and validation.
6. Enter answers to any displayed questions and/or additional details. Unknowns may be left blank. To recalculate, check the separate consent box and tap **Update draft with answers**. This sends the answers and additional details **plus the photos, original description, service and selected rate names again** to Gemini, and each update can use more allowance. The free-tier data-use and information restrictions apply to every update. You can correct earlier answers on a later update. No request is sent merely by typing, and nothing is saved. If there are unsent changes to answers, update them or clear/revert them before opening the editor, so an old price is not mistaken for an updated one. Up to 18 answers are accepted per request; each can contain up to 500 characters, with up to 1,000 additional characters in the extra-details field.
7. A numeric line quantity absent from the person's description, answers and additional details is cleared for manual entry; type known quantities with digits, not just words. Numerical claims absent from that supplied text are also withheld from the summary, price basis, line descriptions/bases and cautions; model questions may still contain numbers. This is a limited check: spelled-out numbers, matching numbers in the wrong context or unit, and unsupported nonnumeric claims may still be wrong. Photos cannot reliably reveal size, measurements, hidden damage, safe access, site restrictions, supplies or all required work. Take measurements or inspect as needed.
8. When a range is available, **Use midpoint as flat draft** opens an unsaved estimate with one flat line: quantity 1 means one job, and its editable price is the midpoint of the AI range, rounded to the nearest cent. The line does not represent a measured area or item quantity. The AI scope, suggested lines and uncertainties go to team-only notes, not separate priced lines. Alternatively, **Review in estimate editor** opens the suggested itemized lines, using verified rate-card matches only when explicitly selected; unmatched prices remain blank. It does not distribute the AI total across line items. Review and edit the scope, quantities, prices, customer notes and total; tax is not automatic. Confirm the review checkbox and explicitly **Save estimate**. If anything changes after checking it, review and check it again. Saving does not send or schedule anything; **View → Copy text / Print / PDF** and scheduling are separate manual actions.

The ballpark, AI summary, questions, answers, extra details, cautions, line explanations and work description are copied to editable **team-only notes** in the editor, not customer-facing notes. Team-only means not shown on the estimate document; all authenticated CRM team accounts have broad access to shared records, and a saved estimate and its team-only notes are included in CRM JSON backups. If the notes exceed 5,000 characters, the app warns before shortening them. Cancelling the suggestion/editor does not create a CRM estimate. AI-selected photos are not saved as job photos or included in backups; they are kept in the open photo dialog's memory for consented follow-ups, then cleared on close. The separate Before / after job-photo feature is unchanged. Existing job quotes are not changed by generating a photo draft.

## Free-tier prerequisite, privacy and access

- **Use and maintain a Gemini API Free-tier project with no active paid billing. The application cannot verify the project's billing status.** Its confirmation setting is an administrator's assertion, not a provider-side spending limit. If the project is paid, linked to active billing, or later upgraded, charges may apply. Check [Google AI Studio billing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/billing) before enabling and monitor it. Do not enable billing, paid tools, credits or automatic purchases without separate approval. Disable this feature and revoke its key if the project no longer meets the Free prerequisite.
- Google lists `gemini-3.8-flash` input and output as free on the Free tier, subject to project/model availability and limits; see current [pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing#gemini-3.8-flash) and [rate limits](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/rate-limits). Photo size, output and each follow-up affect usage; the number of drafts per day is unknown. A limit or temporary capacity problem may block a request. There is no automatic paid upgrade or fallback.
- For unpaid Gemini API services Google says it uses submitted inputs and generated responses to provide, improve and develop products and machine learning, and human reviewers may read, annotate and process them. **Google instructs users not to submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to unpaid services.** See [Google's current terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms#unpaid-services); free API clients made available to users in the EEA, Switzerland or UK are subject to restrictions in those terms. The app sends images inline, not through the File API, and does not request Search, Maps, caching, or other tools. This does not override Google's own retention or data-use policies. Vercel also receives the request; the application code does not intentionally log or persist the photos or provider request.
- The server checks the Supabase bearer token against the CRM project and rejects anonymous or unverified users. **Every authenticated CRM team user can use the feature once enabled.** There is no durable per-user quota or application-level rate limiter; a user with valid CRM access can call the endpoint directly and exhaust the shared allowance. Protect CRM accounts and monitor Gemini usage. The disclosure checkbox is a user-interface confirmation, not a defense against a malicious authenticated user.

## Administrator setup

**Production publication and production configuration require separate approval.** No provider secret should be pasted into the CRM, repository, issue, pull request, screenshots, logs, or chat.

1. Confirm the owner has approved the intended deployment and review the code. Until configured, requests fail closed without reaching Gemini. A Vercel preview can share the **live CRM database**; do not save, delete, or otherwise modify live records as a test.
2. In [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/), select or create a Gemini API project and verify it is on the **Free** tier without an active paid billing account; check its eligibility and current terms. Do not link billing, upgrade, purchase credits or enable paid tools. The app cannot enforce or re-check the billing tier and will not notice a later change.
3. Use [Google AI Studio API keys](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) to create an API key for that Free project. The key is a secret; follow Google's current key-restriction guidance. A consumer Gemini subscription is not the same thing as this Developer API project.
4. In the **primary frontend Vercel project**, privately enter `CRM_GEMINI_API_KEY` as a **Sensitive server-only** variable scoped to the approved deployment environment. Once the owner has verified and committed to maintaining the Free tier, set both `CRM_GEMINI_FREE_TIER_CONFIRMED` and `AI_QUOTES_ENABLED` to the exact value `true` for that environment. Neither switch checks Google's billing settings. Missing/invalid credentials or either switch not exactly `true` prevent a provider request. Do not use `REACT_APP_` variables or expose the key to the browser. Environment changes require a new deployment. For preview-only testing, restrict variables to this working branch's Preview environment, not Production.
5. With the owner's approval, have the owner initiate a small **fictional, non-sensitive photo** live test to verify the response, follow-up and price behavior; inspect Gemini project tier and usage. Test the real phone workflow separately without altering real customer records. Mock tests cannot prove provider JSON compatibility, multi-image behavior, quality, usage, network timeouts or production configuration.
6. Monitor usage and tier status. To stop new use, set `AI_QUOTES_ENABLED` to `false` (and/or remove the Gemini confirmation), redeploy, and revoke the Gemini key in Google AI Studio if an immediate provider-side cutoff is needed. In-flight requests may still finish. If a quota, capacity, setup or provider error occurs, no estimate is saved and there is no automatic retry or paid fallback.

The Vercel function bounds request size, requires explicit consent bound to the Gemini provider and current disclosure version (so a stale Cloudflare form fails closed), sends no server credentials to the browser, independently validates provider output and returns a no-store response. These controls do not eliminate prompt injection, estimation errors, provider retention, spending risk, or the existing CRM's shared-team access model. Use one device at a time for saves and wait for the existing sync indicator; cross-device writes are not transactional.

## Provider switch and older previews

The provider-specific request and response handling is isolated in `frontend/lib/crm-quote-gemini.js`; CRM validation, the estimate editor, and saved estimate records use provider-independent fields. Switching or disabling the provider does not itself delete saved estimates. The new code does not call Cloudflare and has no automatic fallback. Previously issued Cloudflare credentials and older Preview deployments are not automatically removed or revoked; an older preview can still use Cloudflare while its token remains active. Review and revoke unused credentials in Cloudflare if the owner chooses, and do not assume this revision changes old deployments.
